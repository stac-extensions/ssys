## [1.2.0](https://github.com/stac-extensions/ssys/releases/tag/v1.1.1) - 2026-10-28
### Added
- New geometry and illumination fields (`ssys:north_azimuth`, `ssys:solar_longitude`, `ssys:local_true_solar_time`, `ssys:emission_angle`, `ssys:incidence_angle`, `ssys:phase_angle`, `ssys:sub_solar_azimuth`, `ssys:sub_solar_latitude`, `ssys:sub_solar_longitude`, `ssys:sub_spacecraft_latitude`, `ssys:sub_spacecraft_longitude`) and all-fields examples.
